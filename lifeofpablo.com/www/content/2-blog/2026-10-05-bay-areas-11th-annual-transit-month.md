September marked the [Bay Area’s 11th annual Transit Month](https://www.ca.transitmonth.org/bay-area). I didn't realize that there was a month celebrated for Public Transit! Here in San Francisco MUNI hosted an a two-day event called [Muni Heritage Weekend 2026](https://www.sfmta.com/calendar/muni-heritage-weekend-2026) across the street from the Ferry Building! The event was so much fun! I got some cool [MUNI merch](https://themunistore.com/) that was exclusive to that day. 

It was amazing to hop on to see some of the old lineup trolleys and buses that once served San Franciscans. I felt like I was taken back in time by taking one of the old routes on one of the old timers. 

I was told that they fixed up the old trolleys and buses at the MUNI depots which is pretty awesome that they do this in house.
