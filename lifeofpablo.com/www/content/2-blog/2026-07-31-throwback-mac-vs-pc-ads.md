Everyone has commercials they remember. There's a set of commercials that brought me back to life.

I was on YouTube today and I clicked on a complilation video that popped up on my feed. Do you remember the [Get a Mac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Get_a_Mac) compaign by Apple? This video took me back. 

I remember when these commercials when they came out. I would be very excited every time I'd see a new variation of the "beef" and trying to out do each other between the Mac vs. PC. 

Synopsis:
> The Get a Mac advertisements follow a standard template. They open to a plain white background, and a man dressed in casual clothes introduces himself as an Apple Mac computer ("Hello, I'm a Mac."), while a man in a more formal suit-and-tie combination introduces himself as a Microsoft Windows personal computer ("And I'm a PC.").

It's also nice to look back at old Mac models and how iconic they are. I love using old Macs for various purposes. 

Some of my favorites bits are: 
* Star Trek reference
* The animated holiday bits
* The Time Machine

What are some of your favorites?
