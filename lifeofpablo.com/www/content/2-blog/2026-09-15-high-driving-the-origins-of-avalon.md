I'm trying to read more.  I try to read books of different lengths to spend my time better. I also enjoy it when artists release books related to their music. Often, I find myself listening to [Anthony Green](https://www.anthonygreenmusic.com/). I may like his music a little too much. His last tour focused on the re-release of *[Avalon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avalon_(Anthony_Green_album))*. The re-release album is titled *So Long, Avalon*. He released a book that accompanies *[So Long, Avalon](https://anthonygreen.bandcamp.com/album/so-long-avalon).*

> So Long, Avalon is a reworking of Anthony Green's 2008 debut solo album Avalon. It revisits the original songs with new ideas and approaches, shaped by 20 years of live performances, to capture the life they’ve taken on over time.

The book is very personal, beautifully crafted with a mixture of pictures, handwritten lyrics, and plenty of reflections on the album. I recommend reading this book.
